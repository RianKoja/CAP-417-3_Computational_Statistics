### Problem 2 — Binomial Distribution (Slide 20)

> In a manufacturing process, **three** items are selected at random. Each item is inspected and classified as **D** (Defective) or **N** (Non-Defective). The idea is to know about Defective items. So, a Defective item is a success (and Non-Defective is a failure). The items are selected independently. The process produces **25% Defective** items.
>
> - Calculate the **PMF** (Probability Distribution) of the random variable *X* related to this example.
> - Show also the **Probability Histogram**.

### Problem 3 — Uniform Distribution (Slide 28)

> The variance of the discrete uniform distribution is given by:
>

>
> **Why is the denominator 12? Find out.**
